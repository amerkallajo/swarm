# Project Vision

## Mission

Build a reliable, low-volume, high-quality acquisition engine that finds businesses with observable website needs, demonstrates value without pressure, and converts qualified opportunities into profitable web projects.

## Operating principles

1. Qualified opportunities over lead volume.
2. Evidence over inference; unknown is recorded as unknown.
3. Personalization over templates.
4. Durable state over agent memory.
5. Human control at material, reputational, legal, and financial gates.
6. No outreach after opt-out, rejection, or blacklist.
7. No preview work before credible interest and approval.
8. No hidden commercial intent, fake urgency, guaranteed outcomes, or invented familiarity.
9. Bounded cost, retries, and concurrency.
10. Improve from measured reply, interest, preview, and win rates.

## In scope

Ethical public-data discovery, business validation, website audits, transparent scoring, draft generation, approval workflows, Gmail thread tracking, concept preview generation, Netlify deployment, Telegram operations, CRM views, and audit logs.

## Out of scope during initialization

Mass scraping, real outreach, real preview publication, domain purchases, paid resource creation, autonomous pricing/contracting, and sensitive-profession contact.

## Success metrics

Primary: qualified-to-positive-reply rate, interested-to-preview rate, preview-to-win rate, gross profit per qualified lead, opt-out/complaint rate, and evidence accuracy.

Guardrail metrics: duplicate-send count (target 0), post-opt-out sends (0), unapproved outbound actions (0), unsupported claims (0), budget-limit breaches (0), and unreconciled state transitions (0).
