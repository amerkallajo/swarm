# Decisions Required from Amer

## Before Phase 1 external infrastructure

1. Approve the private repository name `swarm` and monorepo stack (TypeScript, pnpm, Turborepo, Next.js, Node worker).
2. Approve creation of a new isolated Supabase development project and preferred region.
3. Choose initial deployment model for the worker after local development (local-only, Railway/Fly/other).
4. Rotate the Telegram token posted in chat, confirm the dedicated bot username, start it, and approve the exact Telegram user/chat IDs.
5. Rotate the Apify token posted in chat and approve its secure storage.

## Before Gmail shadow mode

6. Choose the sending mailbox/domain and provide the agency’s legal sender identity.
7. Approve Google Cloud OAuth setup and initial Gmail scopes (recommend read metadata + drafts, send added later).
8. Define who may approve leads, exact messages, previews, pricing and emergencies.

## Before real discovery/outreach

9. Select the first country/region, language and industry; sensitive professions should not be the first pilot.
10. Approve jurisdiction-specific legal basis, privacy notice, required disclosures, opt-out text, retention and contact schedule after legal review.
11. Set daily/monthly budgets and pilot caps (discovery records, audits, emails, domains, LLM spend, deploys).
12. Approve lead threshold and risk exclusions after reviewing a blinded fixture sample.
13. Decide whether phone/WhatsApp is excluded initially (recommended: email only).

## Before previews and sales

14. Approve concept branding/disclaimer, asset reuse policy, expiration period and whether previews are access-controlled.
15. Define price bands, discount authority, timeline language, contract/payment process and handoff/support boundaries.

## Recommended defaults

Private repo; EU-region isolated Supabase if target data is European; email-only pilot in one non-sensitive B2B local-service industry; five manually selected leads maximum; zero automated follow-ups until review; one follow-up afterward; previews only after explicit interest; 30-day raw-evidence retention unless law/business need requires less or more.
