# Compliance

This is an engineering control framework, not legal advice. Before real outreach, Amer must choose target countries and obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review where necessary.

## Required records

For each source and contact store provenance, collection time, purpose, jurisdiction, applicable terms, lawful-basis assessment, retention period and suppression status. Record the sender’s legal identity and postal/contact details required by local rules.

## Rules

- Use only public sources whose terms and robots/access rules permit the intended collection method.
- Minimize personal data; prefer role/business channels over personal addresses.
- Do not infer sensitive/protected traits or use them for ability-to-pay scoring.
- Identify commercial intent; do not disguise advertising as a personal relationship.
- Honor opt-out immediately and maintain a minimal suppression hash after deletion.
- Do not contact closed, fake, irrelevant, vulnerable, or inappropriate targets.
- Apply conservative contact frequency and local-time windows.
- Do not reuse copyrighted text/images beyond defensible factual/minimal use; previews require asset provenance.
- Support access, correction, deletion, objection and suppression requests.

## Jurisdiction gate

A market configuration must specify permitted channels, lawful basis, required disclosures, opt-out wording, contact-time/frequency limits, data-source restrictions, retention, sensitive-profession rules and approver. Unknown jurisdiction or missing rule means no send.

Special caution is required for EU/UK ePrivacy and GDPR/UK GDPR, US CAN-SPAM plus state privacy/telemarketing law, and profession-specific advertising rules. Email rules differ for corporate versus individual subscribers and by country; SWARM must not generalize one country’s permission to another.

## Suppression hierarchy

Exact address/phone, contact, business/domain, campaign, workspace and global suppression scopes are checked. Opt-out and legal suppression outrank later rediscovery or operator approval.

## Review before production

Approve agency legal identity, privacy notice, data-processing register, vendor DPAs/transfers, target jurisdiction, message disclosures, retention schedule, rights-request process and complaint escalation.
